A TV with no API — volume control over Wi-Fi

A budget smart TV running a stripped-back Android: no Google remote service, no wireless debugging, nothing documented. Every standard route was shut. Scanning the network established what the device actually was, and a test against the live TV proved the obvious route — DLNA volume control — did nothing at all, before a day went into building on it. The answer was a small Python server running on the TV itself and a phone web page that saves to the home screen. One line to install. The whole thing — diagnosis, dead end and build — took under two hours.

The point is not the television. It is the same problem as a supplier portal with no export: no supported way in, and a way in found anyway.

Websites, and the things that run underneath them

We connect to the services you already use, move data between them, and send the reports and emails that someone is currently doing by hand. Built properly, and yours to keep.

01

AI agents and workers

Not a chatbot bolted onto your website. A worker that quietly does the job somebody is currently doing by hand.

The one we get asked for most: a supplier or partner portal with no proper export. Someone logs in every week, clicks through, downloads a spreadsheet and retypes it somewhere useful. We build a worker that signs in on its own schedule, collects the data, files it in a database you own, and builds the report the team actually needs. Logins are held in a secrets store, never sitting in the code.

They also handle the work a fixed script cannot — sorting enquiries, reading documents, drafting the reply, flagging the one that needs a person. We put an approval step wherever the decision matters.

02

AI social media agent

Writes the posts, schedules them, publishes them, then tracks what each one actually did and feeds that back in so the next batch is better. Built for the business where marketing is permanently the thing that slips to next week. You still approve what goes out, if you want to.

03

Automation

The job you repeat every week, handled for you. We connect to the services you already use, move data between them, and send the emails and reports that someone is currently putting together by hand.

04

Reporting and dashboards

Numbers out of a spreadsheet and into something you can read. Figures that update themselves, reports that arrive on schedule, and a flag raised on whatever moved before you go looking for it.

05

Booking and quoting tools

Prices that work themselves out, availability your customer can see, and a quote that lands in your inbox already filled in. Exactly what this site does.

Online shops

A shop you actually own, not a rented page on somebody else’s platform. Product pages, a cart that remembers, and a checkout wired to PayFast, Yoco or Ozow so the money lands in your account. Stock counts down as it sells, your customer gets their order email, and you get one screen that shows what to pack.

Fixing what you already have

A site that loads slowly, looks wrong on a phone, or cannot be found on Google. A contact form that quietly stopped sending and nobody noticed for a month. A security certificate that expired, so every visitor now meets a warning page before they meet you.

We look at what is actually there and tell you honestly which it is. Most of the time it is a few hours of work rather than a rebuild, and we would rather say that than sell you a new site you do not need. If it genuinely has to be rebuilt we will say that too — and you are free to take the advice and go elsewhere with it.
